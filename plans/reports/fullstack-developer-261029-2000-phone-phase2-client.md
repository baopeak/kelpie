6. Chat rendering is a small own renderer (fenced code, plain text, tool rows, permission/ask cards), no markdown library; adapters load lazily per kind.

10. `build.rs` creates `app/dist-phone` when missing (cargo watch) and adds `KELPIE_PHONE_DIST`.

### Pending: docs/user-guide.md (ready to paste, under the Mobile / phone section)
> **The phone page.** Open the pairing link or scan the QR code once; after that the page is an inbox of every session across your projects: sessions that need you come first (longest wait on top), then working, finished-but-unread, idle and stopped ones. Tap a session to read it. A question the agent is waiting on shows as buttons: one tap answers it; if someone else (the desktop or another phone) answered first, the card says who and when. Chat sessions show the conversation and their permission questions; messages to a chat are still written on the computer.
> Typing uses your phone's own keyboard, so Telex, VNI and dictation work; Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line, and a long paste asks first. A key bar adds Esc, Tab, Shift+Tab, arrows, PgUp/PgDn, Ctrl+C, Enter and a one-shot Ctrl. Devices allowed only to read see the screen without a reply box.
> **Bad networks and a busy computer.** The top right always says whether you are live, on a slow connection, reconnecting, offline or whether the computer is busy; what you see then is the last state, dimmed and marked "as of hh:mm:ss". Typing, keys and answers are never queued: they are disabled until the connection is back, and your draft stays on the phone. An answer sent just as the connection dropped is repeated once automatically (the computer ignores a repeat). Settings (tap the indicator): language, light/dark, reconnect, forget this device. Over HTTPS or Tailscale the app can be installed to the home screen and opens even when the computer is off, showing the last state.
